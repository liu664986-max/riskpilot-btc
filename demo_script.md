# 2-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00 - 0:15

Hi, this is RiskPilot BTC, an AI risk cockpit for Bitcoin traders. The product is designed for retail traders who do not need more hype or signals. They need a repeatable risk workflow.

## 0:15 - 0:40

I enter the current BTC market context: price, 24-hour range, support, resistance, funding, and the main macro note. The app generates a daily risk brief that highlights the key risk instead of pretending to predict the market.

## 0:40 - 1:05

The brief flags whether the user is near support, near resistance, in breakdown risk, or in breakout validation. It also produces an action checklist: define max loss first, avoid chasing, and reduce risk below invalidation.

## 1:05 - 1:30

Next is position sizing. The user enters account equity, maximum risk percentage, entry price, and stop price. RiskPilot calculates the largest BTC position that keeps the loss inside the user's plan.

## 1:30 - 1:50

Finally, the trader logs whether they had a plan, defined a stop, respected size, and avoided chasing. The app scores trade discipline, because many BTC losses come from behavior rather than market analysis.

## 1:50 - 2:00

RiskPilot BTC is not a trading bot or signal service. It is a responsible AI product for risk management, with a clear subscription path for individuals and trading communities.
