# RiskPilot BTC 项目简介

## One-liner

RiskPilot BTC is an AI risk cockpit for Bitcoin traders: daily risk briefs, position sizing, and trade discipline reviews without giving financial advice.

## Problem

Most retail BTC traders do not lose only because their market view is wrong. They lose because they chase breakouts, oversize positions, ignore invalidation levels, and trade emotionally during volatility spikes.

## Solution

RiskPilot BTC turns market context into a practical risk workflow:

1. Generate a daily BTC risk brief.
2. Highlight support, resistance, breakout failure, and breakdown risk.
3. Calculate max position size from acceptable loss.
4. Score trade discipline after each trade.
5. Export a clear report for review or sharing.

## Why Now

BTC trades 24/7, volatility is high, and retail traders are overloaded with news, charts, influencers, and leverage products. A lightweight AI risk assistant can reduce decision noise without pretending to predict the market.

## Target Users

- Retail BTC traders
- Crypto trading communities
- Newsletter writers
- Small trading education groups

## MVP Demo Flow

1. User enters current BTC market context.
2. App generates a daily risk brief.
3. User enters account size, entry price, stop price, and max risk percentage.
4. App outputs max position size.
5. User logs a trade and gets a discipline score.

## Business Model

- Individual: $9/week or $29/month
- Community version: $99/month
- API/report generation for trading educators

## Hackathon Positioning

This is not a trading bot and not a signal service. It is a risk-management and behavioral discipline product for volatile markets.
